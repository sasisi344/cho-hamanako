Hero: two silvery Japanese whiting emerging together above the water on separate short droppers of one thin multi-hook line, seen from just above water level.
Supporting: a single arc of droplets where the fish have just cleared the water; a broad, softly blurred strip of pale sand in the background.
Setting: midsummer dawn in the calm sandy shallows of southern Lake Hamana, golden rim light against blue-green water.
Accuracy notes: each Sillago japonica has a very slender, torpedo-shaped silver-beige body roughly six times as long as it is deep, a small pointed mouth and two low separate dorsal fins, with a gently forked tail; keep the two silhouettes distinct with their tails toward the water.
