Hero: one marbled flounder lifting gently off a spring sand flat beside a tidal-channel drop-off, seen at a low three-quarter angle as a thin puff of sand curls under its belly.
Supporting: a modest cluster of ragworms on a hook just ahead of the fish; a tiny scatter of pale pink cherry petals floating on the surface above.
Setting: March to May in the brackish shallows of southern Lake Hamana, warm spring light filtering into cool water.
Accuracy notes: the marbled flounder is an oval, strongly flattened right-eyed flatfish, both eyes on its mottled brown upper side, with continuous fin fringes and a distinct tail.
